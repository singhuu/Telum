# Changes

The release workflow copies the section of each version into its release notes. Add a section before you push the version tag.

## 0.1.0

The first public release.

- A home screen for Android TV with large app tiles. Hold OK on a tile to move it or hide it.
- A first-start setup. Select your apps and put them in order. Then select **Recommended**, **Light**, or your own settings.
- **Run setup again** in **Control centre**. The changes apply only when you save them.
- A question at the end of setup that makes Telum the Home app. **Control centre** › **Telum** also has **Make Telum your Home**.
- A short star intro at the start of setup and at the first start after a restart. OK, Back, or an arrow skips it. **Intro sound** turns its sound on or off.
- Backgrounds that follow the highlighted app, with color profiles for 33 services.
- Optional Live Activities, music controls, a dim music display, artist photos, and weather.
- **Control centre** with shortcuts to Android settings.
- An installation procedure for AI agents, a manual installation guide, and a troubleshooting guide.
